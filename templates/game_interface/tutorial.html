How to Play

Getting Started

  1. Create an account or log in
  2. Choose a game from the Games page
  3. Start a new game session
  4. Share the game link with your friends

Playing the Game

  1. Click on the game piece you want to move
  2. Click on the destination where you want to place it
  3. Confirm your move
  4. The robot will execute your move on the physical board
